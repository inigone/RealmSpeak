Inigone's Alternate Guilds

The guilds are a new type of dwelling on the board. They are initially placed as chits, and appear when revealed, much like the
two campfires. Once revealed, they stay revealed for the remainder of the game (except for the Theives' Guild which must be
discovered in the same manner as treasure sites.) They are placed in a random clearing which connects back to the Borderland.

All the guilds allow TRADE, so a character can buy/sell as if they were interacting with a native leader. At the start of the
game, each guild receives items to trade according to each guild's setup sheet box. To non-guild members, including those who
have not yet completed the join requirement (Pledges), the guild's relationship with any character is UNFRIENDLY, but for members
of the guild, this relationship improves as you advance through the ranks:

    LEVEL   TITLE        GUILD RELATIONSHIP   HIDDEN AT GUILD   MEMBER?
    -----   ----------   ------------------   ---------------   -------------
      0     Pledge       Unfriendly           No                No
      1     Initiate     Neutral              No                Yes
      2     Apprentice   Neutral              Yes               Yes
      3     Journeyman   Friendly             Yes               Yes
      4     Master       Ally                 Yes               Yes

Once a character joins a guild (as an Initiate), they cannot voluntarily leave the guild for the duration of the game. The
character can then complete guild quests (unique to each guild) in order to attain higher ranks. Each completed guild quest
will advance the character's guild level by 1.

Each level the guild member attains above level 1 (Initiate) also gives them the opportunity to gain a relationship level with one
of two possible native groups*, as determined by the guild type. The same native group does not need to be chosen each time.

At each new level acheived, a guild member will gain benefits and access to services specific to each guild, as described below.

Each guild may have only a single member at Master level.  A level 3 guild member may advance to level 4 only if the unique 
guild chit is not already owned by another member of the guild.  A level 4 guild member who is killed, or is demoted in the guild,
or loses guild membership for any reason, causes the unique guild chit they own to be immediately returned to the guild, and can
then be distributed to the next level 3 member which completes a guild quest to advance them to level 4.

Guild-members at their guild location are considered to be in a dwelling.  Non-guild-members are not.

Level 2+ Guild-members at their guild location gain hidden status at the start of evening.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

FIGHTERS' GUILD

Join Requirement (Pledge): Kill the HQ leader of any native group other than Soldiers or Guards.
  Pledge Benefit: Gain an extra ALERT phase per turn for 2 weeks after any full day spent at the guild.

Member Benefits:
Level 1+: Gain an extra ALERT phase per turn.  (Does not expire.)
Level 2+: Additional REST phase per turn.  Improve native relationship.
Level 3+: Sharpness stars unaffected by armor.  Improve native relationship.
Level 4:  Reduce any selected FIGHT chit by one effort asterisk.  Improve native relationship.

Member Services:
Level 2+ Service: Each REST phase at guild provides 3 REST actions.
Level 3+ Service: Free repair of all damaged armor you own if you spend an entire day at the guild.

(*) Native Groups: Soldiers, Guard.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

MAGIC USERS' GUILD

Join Requirement (Pledge): Learn any new spell.
  Pledge Benefit: Gain a -1 (max) Reading Runes Table DRM for 2 weeks after any full day spent (training) at the guild.

Member Benefits:
Level 1+: Gain a -1 (max) Reading Runes Table DRM.  (Does not expire.)
Level 2+: Additional ENCHANT phase per turn.  Improve native relationship.
Level 3+: Enchant one chit for free (per day).  Improve native relationship.
Level 4:  Change 1 MAGIC chit to any other type.  Improve native relationship.

Member Services:
Level 2+: Cancel any one spell (if desired) during an overnight stay at the guild.
Level 3+: Cancel any one curse during an overnight stay at the guild.

(*) Native Groups: Woodfolk, Bashkars.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

THIEVES' GUILD

Join Requirement (Pledge): Steal 1 treasure from any native HQ.
  Pledge Benefit: Gain a -1 (max) Steal Attempt Table DRM for 2 weeks after any full day spent at the guild.

Member Benefits:
Level 1+ Benefit: Gain a -1 (max) Steal Attempt Table DRM. (Does not expire.) 
Level 2+ Benefit: Additional HIDE phase per turn.  Improve native relationship.
Level 3+ Benefit: May target other charaters with STEAL actions.  Improve native relationship.
Level 4  Benefit: Reduce any selected MOVE chit by one effort asterisk.  Improve native relationship.

Member Services:
Level 2+ Service: Spend a day at the guild to learn the location of all unenchanted tile hidden paths within a 2 tile radius
                  from the guild.
Level 3+ Service: Spend a day at the guild to learn the location of all unenchanted tile secret passages within a 2 tile radius
                  from the guild.
Level 4 Service:  Spend a day at the guild to learn how to unlock any locked treasure or treasure site.

(*) Native Groups: Rogues, Murkers.
